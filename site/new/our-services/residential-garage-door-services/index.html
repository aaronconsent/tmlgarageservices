Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a garage door installation take?

Most residential garage door installations are completed in one day, depending on the size of the door and any additional work required.

Can I keep my existing garage door opener?

In many cases, yes. We will inspect your current opener to ensure it is compatible with your new garage door and recommend replacement only if necessary.

Should I choose an insulated garage door?

Insulated garage doors help improve energy efficiency, reduce outside noise, and provide a quieter operation. They're a great option for attached garages and homes in the Houston area.

Do you remove the old garage door?

Yes. We safely remove and dispose of your existing garage door and hardware as part of the installation service.
