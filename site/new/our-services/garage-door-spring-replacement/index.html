Fast, Safe & Professional Garage Door Spring Replacement

A broken garage door spring can leave your garage door stuck, heavy, or unsafe to operate. At TML GARAGE DOOR SERVICES, we provide fast and reliable garage door spring replacement for homeowners throughout Houston and the surrounding areas.

Signs You Need Garage Door Spring Replacement

If you notice any of these problems, your garage door spring may need to be replaced:

  • Garage door won't open or only opens a few inches
  • Loud bang coming from the garage
  • Broken or separated spring
  • Garage door feels unusually heavy
  • Opener struggles or stops working
  • Crooked or uneven garage door
  • Door closes too quickly
  • Visible gaps in the spring

Do not attempt to replace garage door springs yourself. Springs are under extreme tension and can cause serious injury if handled improperly!

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does garage door spring replacement take?

Most spring replacement jobs are completed in about 1–2 hours, depending on the type of garage door and spring system.

Can I replace only one spring?

If your garage door has two springs, replacing both at the same time is often recommended because they usually wear out at a similar rate.

Can I use my garage door with a broken spring?

No. Operating the garage door with a broken spring can damage the opener and create a safety hazard.

How long do garage door springs last?

Standard springs typically last around 10,000 cycles. High-cycle springs can last significantly longer with proper maintenance.
