Frequently Asked Questions

Should I repair or replace my garage door opener?

If your opener is older, frequently breaks down, or lacks modern safety features, replacing it may be the better long-term solution. We'll inspect your system and recommend the best option.

How long does opener installation take?

Most garage door opener installations are completed within 2–4 hours.

Can I control my garage door from my phone?

Yes. Many modern garage door openers include Wi-Fi connectivity and smartphone control, allowing you to open, close, and monitor your garage door remotely.

Do you program remotes and keypads?

Yes. We can program new or existing remotes, wireless keypads, and compatible smart devices.
