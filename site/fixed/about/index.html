About Us

At TML Garage Door Services, we're committed to providing dependable, high-quality garage door solutions for homeowners and businesses throughout the Houston area. With years of hands-on experience, our team delivers expert craftsmanship, honest pricing, and outstanding customer service on every job.

We specialize in garage door repair, broken spring replacement, garage door opener repair and installation, new garage door installation, cable and roller replacement, off-track door repair, preventive maintenance, and commercial garage door services. Whether it's a minor repair or a complete garage door replacement, we use quality parts and proven techniques to ensure long-lasting results.

Our goal is simple: deliver fast, reliable service and complete every job right the first time. We understand that a malfunctioning garage door can be stressful and disruptive, which is why we offer prompt response times, same-day service when available, and clear, upfront pricing with no hidden fees.

At TML Garage Door Services, we treat every customer like family. From your first call to the completion of your service, you can expect professionalism, honesty, and a commitment to your satisfaction.
