Booking questions

    
How fast can you get here?
Same-day appointments are available most days, including weekends at no extra charge. If your door is stuck or a spring is broken, call (832) 887-8747 and we'll get you on the schedule right away.

Pricing is upfront — your technician gives you the full price before any work begins, so there are no surprises. A complete garage door tune-up and safety inspection is $69.
