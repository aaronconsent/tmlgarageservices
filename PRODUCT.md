# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners in The Woodlands / Conroe / Spring / greater Houston with a garage door problem — most urgently a door that won't open, a snapped spring, or a dead opener. They arrive on mobile, often stressed, comparing 2–3 local companies from a Google search. Secondary: commercial property managers needing overhead door or gate service, and warm researchers comparing brands (LiftMaster vs Genie) before an install. The demo has a second audience: the TML owner, who must see his own brand elevated, not replaced.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for TML Garage Services, a family garage-door repair and installation company. Success = the visitor calls. The redesign exists to quadruple conversions over the current Webflow template: every page must move a stressed mobile visitor from "is this company legit?" to tapping the phone number in under 30 seconds.

## Positioning

The Houston-area garage door crew that answers, shows up same-day, and quotes honestly — a real local family company, not a dispatch-farm lead reseller.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: tap-to-call (832) 887-8747 — persistent sticky call bar on mobile, phone number treated as the hero element sitewide.
- Secondary CTA: "Book online" → Google Calendar scheduler (calendar.app.google/juqN1UNcknf4KZ9v9) for after-hours visitors and planners.
- The line a visitor remembers: "Broken door? We're local and we answer."
- Belief ladder: (1) they fix my exact problem → (2) they serve my area → (3) they're real people, licensed, been around → (4) others trusted them and it went well → (5) calling is easy and won't commit me to anything.
- Proof on hand: Angi 2019 Super Service Award, HomeAdvisor Top Rated + Screened & Approved + 3-year badges, Trustmary review widget, Facebook/Instagram with real job photos, real project galleries (kitchen + outdoor projects from their home-improvement history), brand partnerships (LiftMaster, Genie, Chamberlain, Craftsman).

## Brand Personality

Sturdy, straight-talking, local. The voice of a foreman who picks up on the second ring: confident, unhurried, zero marketing perfume. Emotions to evoke: relief ("these people handle it"), trust ("they're from here"), urgency handled calmly.

## Anti-references

- The current site itself: generic Webflow "Builderz" template — interchangeable hero, stock template blog, fake template team names.
- National lead-gen mills (HomeAdvisor landing pages, "$29 service call!" banner sites) — coupon-clutter erodes trust.
- AI-landing-page grammar: cream/beige surfaces, tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text.
- Booked Job's caution-tape orange/yellow (sibling brand in this portfolio — no convergence).

## Design Principles

1. The phone number is the design. Every fold answers "why call TML" and makes calling one thumb-tap away.
2. Their brand, elevated. Evolve the olive/charcoal logo identity into a committed deep-olive + high-vis palette; the owner should recognize his company instantly.
3. Mobile is the site. Design at 375px first; desktop is the enhancement.
4. Proof over promises. Badges, reviews, real job photos, and brand logos do the persuading; copy stays plain.
5. Fast is a feature. A stressed visitor on cell data gets a usable page in one second — no template bloat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, 3:1 large text, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, semantic landmarks, 44px minimum touch targets on all tap-to-call elements.
